A Letter From My Heart 💌

My dear Kukushaye,

I want to say this clearly, without hiding behind beautiful words: I love you, Kukushaye. I love you deeply, sincerely, and with a tenderness that is difficult to fit into an ordinary message. This is not just a passing thought or a pretty sentence for a screen. It is what my heart has been trying to tell you.

You matter to me more than I have always known how to express. When I think of you, I feel affection, admiration, and a longing to be honest about what is in my heart. Loving you has made me see both the beautiful parts of my feelings and the parts of myself that still need patience and growth.

I am not writing this to pressure you or to ask you to give me an answer. I am writing because I do not want the main truth to get lost: my feelings for you are real, and I love you. I respect your heart and your freedom, and I want my love to be expressed with kindness rather than pressure. You deserve peace, happiness, and the freedom to choose what is right for you.

If I have ever been quiet when I should have spoken, or confused when I should have been clear, I am learning from that. I am learning that meaningful love is not only about longing; it is also about patience, respect, responsibility, and becoming someone who can offer care without trying to control the outcome.

There are memories that make the heart soft, words that stay with us, and people who remind us to look more honestly at ourselves. For me, you have been part of that journey. I can appreciate what you have meant to me without pretending that I know what tomorrow will bring.

So this is not a promise that life must unfold in one particular way. It is a quiet truth from me: I wish you happiness, safety, calm, and a love that makes you feel valued. I hope you never have to shrink your light to make anyone comfortable. I hope you are surrounded by people who see your worth and treat your heart with care.

If you remember one thing from this letter, let it be this: I love you, Kukushaye. I am not asking you to carry my feelings or to promise me anything. I only wanted you to know the truth directly from me. You are your own person, with your own heart and choices, and I will respect that while being honest about mine.

With sincerity, warmth, and a heart that is learning to love more wisely.

I love you, Kukushaye. Truly. ♥
